## 2. Phase 5 UI Polish & Ergonomics (`tests/phase5_polish_and_ergonomics.rs`)
- **Unified Raag Branding:** Replaced YouTube Music logo and split Resonance labels with an original Raag acoustic wave emblem and unified Raag branding across Desktop, Android, Android Auto, MPRIS/SMTC, Updater, and Web preview host.
- **Persistent Mini-Player & Desktop Player Bar:** Interactive Desktop top-edge seek slider and track context menu; mobile mini-player stacked strictly above bottom nav with zero overlap.
- **Now Playing (Priority #1):** Stream/offline telemetry badge (`Opus 251 • Proxy` ↔ `Offline Cache`), Explicit (`E`) badge, and accessible `>= 48dp` Volume & Mute strip.
- **Synced Lyrics (Priority #2):** Honest `Synced • <provider>` vs `Lyrics • <provider>` badge, active line progress counter (`Line X / Y`), and explicitly labeled `Seek` `-5s` / `+5s` controls.
- **Queue (Priority #3):** Tabular-nums total queue runtime summary, atomic `PlayQueue` playback, non-destructive `Clear Up Next`, bulk `Save Queue` playlist creation, and quick `Shuffle`/`Repeat` pills.
- **Library, Local Files, Entity Pages & Settings:** Direct Album/Artist entity routing across Home/Explore/Search/Library, virtualized Local Files track list with working Play Next, collapsible `Advanced & Diagnostics` and cookie paste drawers in Settings, and global dismissible status toast.
